Teaching

My teaching integrates criminological theory with spatial methodologies and quantitative data analysis. I aim to equip students—from undergraduates learning fundamentals to graduate researchers developing advanced studies—with critical thinking and computational skills to rigorously examine crime patterns and urban problems.
