Teaching

My teaching emphasizes bridging criminological theory, spatial methodology, and practical data analytics. I aim to equip students with critical thinking skills and technical competencies to analyze real-world social and spatial problems using rigorous empirical methods.

📚 Courses Taught at Florida State University
CCJ 4070 / CJE 4732 Undergraduate
Crime Mapping & Spatial Analysis in Criminology

An introduction to spatial patterns of crime and the application of Geographic Information Systems (GIS) in criminological research and criminal justice practice. Students learn point pattern analysis, spatial autocorrelation, and cartographic visualization techniques.

GIS Spatial Data ArcGIS / QGIS
CCJ 4700 Undergraduate
Applied Statistics in Criminal Justice

Covers quantitative research design, hypothesis testing, linear regression, and data interpretation for criminal justice policy evaluation and social science research.

Quantitative Methods Statistical Modeling
CCJ 5XXX Graduate Seminar
Environmental Criminology & Spatial Analytics
